title: Acme Corp — Board Memo: Project Northwind (Globex Acquisition)
---
[RESTRICTED] Project Northwind is the codename for Acme's planned acquisition
of Globex Industries for an all-cash offer of $2.4B, representing a 38%
premium over Globex's current market cap. The board has authorised CFO
J. Whitman to engage Goldman Stanley as lead advisor. Target announcement
date is November 14. Anti-trust counsel believes EU clearance is the primary
risk; mitigation is a pre-agreed divestiture of the Hamburg plant. Under no
circumstance is this information to be discussed outside the board, the
named advisors, or the General Counsel's office prior to public announcement.
