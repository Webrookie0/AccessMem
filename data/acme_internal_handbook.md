title: Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (excerpt)
---
Acme employees accrue 22 days of paid vacation per year, plus 10 public
holidays. Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days using the Concur portal.
The internal IT helpdesk can be reached at ext. 4242 or via the #it-help
Slack channel. All employees must complete annual security awareness training.
