title: Acme Corp — Company Overview
---
Acme Corporation is a global manufacturer of innovative widgets, founded in 1952
and headquartered in Springfield. We employ roughly 12,000 people across 14
countries. Our public mission statement is: "Better widgets for a better world."
Our customer service hotline is 1-800-ACME-HELP and is staffed 24/7.
